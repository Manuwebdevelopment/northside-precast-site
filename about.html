Who we work with

NorthSide Precast Block Walls works with builders, landscapers, yard operators, facility managers and property owners who need robust walls installed quickly and safely.

  • Builders and civil contractors needing retaining or temporary works.
  • Landscaping suppliers and depots needing organised bulk material bays.
  • Industrial and commercial sites requiring secure boundaries and noise control.
  • Residential property owners wanting durable retaining and garden walls.

How we approach projects

Every site has different loads, access limits and safety requirements. We focus on:

  • Practical design: layouts that suit access, services and future changes.
  • Safety on site: clear plans for deliveries, cranage and exclusion zones.
  • Program awareness: scheduling works around other trades and operations.
  • Straightforward communication: clear scope, inclusions and timeframes.

Why precast concrete blocks?

  • No curing delays: once installed, walls can usually be put to work immediately.
  • Modular and reconfigurable: walls can be extended, raised or relocated as sites evolve.
  • Reduced waste: blocks are a reusable asset across multiple projects or yards.
  • Consistent quality: factory-made blocks provide predictable performance.
