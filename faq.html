Precast block basics

What size precast concrete blocks do you use?
We primarily work with 600 x 600 x 1200 precast concrete blocks that are engineered for structural performance and modular stacking.
Where do you operate?
We are based in Mount Kuring-gai, NSW and regularly service Mount Colah, Berowra, Hornsby, Asquith, Wahroonga, the Upper North Shore and wider Sydney Metro by arrangement.
How fast can a precast block wall be installed?
Because there is no curing time, many small retaining or storage bay walls can be installed within a day once site preparation and access are confirmed.
Are precast block walls suitable for residential properties?
They are a great option for residential retaining walls, garden terraces, driveways and property boundaries where you want a robust, long-lasting solution.

Engineering, site preparation and installation

Do I need full engineering for my wall?
All structural walls should be designed or verified by a qualified engineer, particularly retaining walls, flood barriers and high walls. We can work with your engineer or help you connect with one.
What kind of site preparation is required?
Most projects only require a compacted, level base capable of supporting the wall loads. Compared with in-situ concrete, site preparation is typically simpler and faster.
Can the walls be moved or extended later?
Yes. One of the key advantages of modular precast blocks is that they can be dismantled, reconfigured or extended as your site requirements change.
Can you work on live commercial or industrial sites?
Yes. Our installation methods are suited to live sites, and we plan deliveries and crane or forklift movements to minimise disruption to your operations.
Do you arrange delivery and cranage of the blocks?
Yes. We coordinate block supply, delivery and cranage or lifting equipment as part of the installation scope, unless you prefer to manage supply yourself.

Licensing, insurance and quoting

Are you licensed and insured?
Yes, we hold the appropriate construction licences and public liability insurance for the work we perform. Specific licence and policy details can be provided with your quote.
Can you build temporary walls for construction sites?
Yes. Our modular blocks are ideal for temporary construction walls, partitions and safety barriers that can be removed or reconfigured later.
