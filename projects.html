Information we’ll ask for

To provide a useful project proposal, we typically ask for:

  • Site address and access constraints.
  • Wall purpose, approximate length and height.
  • Any available plans, sections or engineering.
  • Preferred timeframes and any site restrictions.

Sharing this early helps us confirm whether a precast block wall is the right solution and what installation will involve.
