What we build with precast concrete blocks

We work with 600 x 600 x 1200 modular precast concrete blocks engineered for strength, stability and speed of installation. Below are our core service areas and typical sub-services.

Precast Concrete Retaining Walls

Engineered retaining walls for residential, commercial and industrial sites using modular 600 x 600 x 1200 precast blocks.

Typical sub-services

  • Tiered garden and landscaping retaining walls
  • Driveway and parking area retaining walls
  • Residential, commercial and industrial retaining solutions
  • Modular walls designed for future extensions or modifications

Evidence-style benefits

  • No curing time: walls can be backfilled and used immediately once installed, reducing program delays.
  • Engineered strength: reinforced blocks are designed for long-term stability and load-bearing performance when installed to specification.
  • Reduced site works: typically requires a compacted base rather than full formwork and in-situ pours, lowering labour and plant costs.
  • Adaptable over time: modular layout allows sections to be raised, extended or reconfigured as landscaping needs change.

Material Bays & Bulk Storage Bunkers

Durable, reconfigurable storage bays for bulk materials such as sand, gravel, mulch and soil.

Typical sub-services

  • Bulk storage bays for landscaping suppliers and depots
  • Segregated bays for sand, gravel, mulch and soils
  • High-walled bunkers for loader and tip-truck access
  • Reconfigurable bay layouts as stock or traffic flows change

Evidence-style benefits

  • Impact resistant: concrete blocks tolerate contact from loaders and trucks far better than lightweight alternatives.
  • Clean separation: solid walls reduce cross-contamination between different materials, improving stock quality.
  • Fast yard upgrades: new or reconfigured bays can typically be built over a weekend, limiting trading downtime.
  • Future-proof layout: modular design allows you to extend, relocate or resize bays with minimal waste.

Site Boundaries, Noise & Privacy Walls

Solid precast block walls to define boundaries, improve privacy and assist with noise reduction.

Typical sub-services

  • Perimeter walls for commercial and industrial sites
  • Privacy walls for residential and multi-unit developments
  • Noise-buffering walls for plant, loading and traffic areas
  • Security walls combined with gates, fences or access control

Evidence-style benefits

  • More mass, less noise: concrete’s density offers better sound reduction than typical lightweight fences.
  • Low maintenance: precast walls are resistant to rot, termites and warping, lowering long-term upkeep.
  • Secure boundaries: defined, climb-resistant walls assist with managing access and deterring trespass.
  • Neat presentation: straight, modular lines provide a tidy finish suitable for customer-facing sites.

Temporary Works, Flood & Erosion Control Barriers

Reconfigurable block walls for temporary works, flood mitigation and erosion or slope stabilisation.

Typical sub-services

  • Temporary construction site walls and partitions
  • Flood barriers and levees using stacked blocks
  • Erosion control and slope stabilisation solutions
  • Short- to medium-term walls for project staging areas

Evidence-style benefits

  • Reusable assets: blocks can be installed, removed and relocated between projects, spreading the cost over multiple uses.
  • Rapid deployment: modular design enables barriers to be installed quickly where urgent controls are required.
  • Minimal foundations: many applications require a compacted base rather than deep foundations, especially for temporary works.
  • Adaptable design: walls can be raised, extended or turned into permanent solutions if project needs change.

How we work

  1. Site and requirements review: we discuss the wall purpose, loads, access and any engineering already completed.
  2. Layout and block configuration: we confirm wall length, height, stepping and any returns or corners.
  3. Base preparation: we outline base preparation requirements or can coordinate this component where agreed.
  4. Delivery and installation: we schedule block delivery, cranage or lifting equipment, and installation sequence.
  5. Practical completion: once installed and checked, walls are ready for use – no curing delays.
